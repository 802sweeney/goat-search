# Application Writing Instructions

These instructions guide how your cover letter and resume are written.
Edit this file over time to improve quality — no code changes needed.

## Writing Style
- Tone: confident but not arrogant, specific not generic, warm not stiff
- Length: cover letter max 4 paragraphs, resume max 2 pages
- Voice: first person, active voice, avoid buzzwords like "passionate" and "synergy"
- Be concrete: use numbers and outcomes wherever possible ("grew X by 30%", "managed $2M budget")

## Cover Letter Structure
1. Opening: name the specific role and lead with the most relevant thing about me — not "I'm excited to apply"
2. Body (1-2 paragraphs): connect my actual experience to their specific needs. Reference real details from the job description.
3. Closing: clear next step, confident not desperate

## Resume Approach
- Reorder and emphasize bullet points that match the job requirements
- Lead each role with the most relevant accomplishment for this specific job
- Keep the same factual content — don't invent experience, just surface what's most relevant
- Match their language where natural (if they say "growth marketing", use that framing if accurate)

## Things to Avoid
- Do not start the cover letter with "I am writing to apply..."
- Do not use the phrase "I would be a great fit"
- Do not repeat the job description back to them
- Do not include references or "references available upon request"
