Job Funnel — Last 30 Days

Screened, Applied, and Interviewed all counted within the same 30-day window, so the stages are directly comparable. A job that made it all the way to Interviewing still counts toward Screened and Applied here, not just its current stage. This can't show a true lifetime total — see Lifetime Stats below for the permanent counters instead.

Lifetime Stats

Permanent counters, tracked going forward from when this was added (2026-09-28) — unlike the 30-day funnel above, these never age out. Reviewed/Presented/Rejected only cover the automated sweeps (the overwhelming majority of real volume); manually captured or ad-hoc-added jobs aren't counted here. There's no way to recover accurate totals from before this existed — job records get purged after 30 days, and that's the only place this detail ever lived.
